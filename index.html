about_findeubug()

/* what is this thing? */

FINDEUBUG is a two-player competitive debugging game played over your local network. Both players get the same broken program, and the first one to find and fix the bug wins the round. Sharpen your code-reading skills while racing a friend on the same Wi-Fi.

> join_or_host()

Join an open game, or host a match for two other players on your LAN.

> race_to_fix()

Same snippet, same bug, same clock. Spot the problem and patch it before your rival.

> custom_snippets()

Out of the 300 built-in programs? Add your own buggy code and challenge friends.

faq()

/* frequently asked questions */

Two players compete head-to-head. To host a match you need three devices in total: the host plus two players.

No. FINDEUBUG runs over a local network, so every device just needs to be on the same Wi-Fi or LAN.

The built-in set includes 300 (C++) short programs with a single hidden bug each. Different difficulty levels cover common mistakes like off-by-one errors, wrong operators, and bad conditions.

Whoever finds and fixes the bug first earns the most points for the round. Faster and more accurate answers score higher, and totals appear on the leaderboard.

Yes. Use the custom_snippets() menu to add your own buggy programs and play them in your next match.
